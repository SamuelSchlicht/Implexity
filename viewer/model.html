Regions, loads and boundary conditions
First select an area on the model. Then choose what acts there, enter its value and units, and review the assignment before applying it. A region describes where; a load or boundary condition describes what happens there.

      
For heat input, choose Heat flux and select the surface; enter the flux in W/m² in the viewport tool. For a fixed temperature or support on an existing region, use the guided cards below. Flow inlets and outlets belong to the CFD provider's Boundary faces setup. Creating a region alone does not apply a physical condition.
